# The palettes on the Options window's Display page, besides the game's own.
#
# A palette gives the game's named colours new values -- the colours the map is
# drawn in when it is drawn in letters, the sidebar, the messages, the lists --
# and the front end's own roles: the health bar's good, fair and poor, and the
# mana, food and experience bars.  A colour a palette leaves out keeps the
# game's value.  Tile sets have their colours in their sheets and keep them.
#
# Checked by scripts/palettes/check-palettes.py, which fails when a palette
# loses a difference the game gives a meaning to: hit points going from green
# to yellow to red, good and bad effects, a monster's health bar.  The
# colour-blind palette is checked under simulated protanopia, deuteranopia and
# tritanopia too.  Change a colour here, run that, and look at the result in
# the game -- the numbers say whether a pair can be told apart, not whether
# the palette is pleasant.
#
#   palette  <key>  <label>
#   colour   <the game's name>  #rrggbb
#   role     good|fair|poor|mana|food|exp  #rrggbb
#
# Fields are separated by tabs.
