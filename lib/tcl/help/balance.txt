H	How Balance Differs
M	how-balance-differs
T	h1	How Balance Differs
T	h1	\n
T	p0	ZangbandTK is built on Angband 4.2 and rebuilds the character of Zangband, a variant whose development stopped in 2005. It matches neither of its ancestors exactly. This chapter states plainly how it differs from both, so that players arriving from either know what to expect.
T	p0	\n
M	monsters-die-sooner
T	h2	Monsters die sooner
T	h2	\n
T	p0	Every monster in ZangbandTK carries 
T	p0 strong	73% of Angband’s hit points
T	p0	 and 
T	p0 strong	50% of its armour class
T	p0	.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	This is the single change that makes the game feel different, and it is the only mechanism by which ZangbandTK moves faster than vanilla Angband. Monsters die sooner and are easier to hit, so encounters resolve faster and turn over more quickly. They also kill you sooner: a fight you would have survived in Angband by grinding it out is now decided in fewer turns, in whichever direction it was going.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	Those figures are not invented. They are what Zangband did, measured across the 450 monsters that Zangband 2.7.5 and Angband 2.8.1 have in common — Zangband’s monsters carried a median 0.73× the hit points and 0.50× the armour class of the Angband release it forked from.
T	p0	\n
M	why-scale-rather-than-import
T	h2	Why scale rather than import
T	h2	\n
T	p0	ZangbandTK does not use Zangband’s own per-monster numbers for creatures that already exist in Angband. It scales Angband 4.2’s numbers instead.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	Angband spent twenty-five years tuning the relationships 
T	p0 em	between
T	p0	 its monsters — which are dangerous for their depth, which are pushovers, which punch above their weight. Importing Zangband’s 2005 values wholesale would have discarded all of that to no benefit, because those relationships are not what makes Zangband distinctive. Scaling keeps Angband’s relative balance and adopts Zangband’s absolute lethality.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	Monsters that exist only in Zangband have no Angband counterpart to scale, so they are placed on Angband 4.2’s own depth curve, using Zangband’s numbers only as a signal of their relative role — whether a creature is meant to be tanky, fragile or fast for its depth.
T	p0	\n
M	experience-is-unchanged
T	h2	Experience is unchanged
T	h2	\n
T	p0	ZangbandTK awards experience exactly as Angband 4.2 does. There is no multiplier, and levelling thresholds are untouched.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	This surprises people who remember Zangband as a faster game, so it is worth stating why. Zangband appears at first glance to award roughly twenty times more experience per monster, but this is an artefact of how the two games store the value. Angband stores experience 
T	p0 em	per monster level
T	p0	 and awards 
T	p0 code	value × level ÷ player level
T	p0	; Zangband stored the 
T	p0 em	total
T	p0	 and awarded 
T	p0 code	value ÷ player level
T	p0	. Once that difference is accounted for, Zangband’s experience per kill is within 2% of Angband’s — a median ratio of 0.98 across the monsters they share.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	Zangband’s levelling threshold table is byte-identical to Angband’s, and its race experience factors are slightly 
T	p0 em	higher
T	p0	, meaning marginally slower levelling. There is simply no faster progression to inherit. What made Zangband feel quick was monsters dying sooner, which is the change described above.
T	p0	\n
M	tuning-it-yourself
T	h2	Tuning it yourself
T	h2	\n
T	p0	Both scalars live in 
T	p0 code	lib/gamedata/constants.txt
T	p0	 and take effect on restart, with no rebuild required:
T	p0	\n
T	pre0	lethality:hit-points:73\n
T	pre0 prelast	lethality:armor-class:50\n
T	p0	They are percentages of each monster’s base value. 
T	p0 strong	Setting both to 100 gives behaviour identical to vanilla Angband 4.2
T	p0	, which is a supported configuration — useful for comparison, and for players who want ZangbandTK’s content at Angband’s pacing.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	Lower values make the game faster and more lethal in both directions. There is no upper limit, but note that hit points floor at 1 and armour class at 0, so very low percentages compress the difference between weak and strong monsters.
T	p0	\n
M	savefiles
T	h2	Savefiles
T	h2	\n
T	p0	ZangbandTK does not load Angband or Zangband savefiles, and never will. The player model, level persistence and world layout all diverge too far for a converted character to be meaningful. Savefiles carry the tag 
T	p0 code	ZZK1
T	p0	, and the game will decline to open anything else rather than load it partially.
T	p0	\n
