T	p0	Nobody chooses them. You are not told at birth which eight you drew; you find out by watching what the world makes of you.

T	h2	Where this came from
T	h2	\n
T	p0	Topi Ylinen wrote the virtue system for Zangband in 1998, as an Ultima IV-style measure of how a character behaves. It was never finished. By the time development stopped in 2005 there were 168 places in the source that 
T	p0 em	wrote
T	p0	 a virtue and not one that read a virtue back, and the knowledge-screen entry that would have shown them to the player was commented out with the note 
T	p0 em	“Display virtues option is always left out”
T	p0	. Every character in Zangband carried eight counters that moved with everything they did, and nothing — not one line, in seven years — ever looked at them.
T	p0	\n
T	p0	The selection is Zangband’s, near enough unchanged, because it is the part that did real work. The two things that read a virtue are new, and so is the rule that every virtue a character can be given must be one their play can move: the original had no such rule and would not have needed one, since nothing was reading.
