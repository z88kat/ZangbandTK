# File: dungeon.txt
#
# The dungeons of the world (WLD-14).
#
# Angband has one dungeon and depth is the only thing that tells level 40 from
# level 4.  Here there are several, each covering a range of depths, each with
# a floor of its own, and each with a bottom you have to leave it to get past.
# That is what makes finding a new one worth the walk.
#
# These are Amber's places, per DEC-30: Zelazny's geography rather than the
# assortment of Lovecraft, Tolkien and general fantasy that Zangband's own
# dungeon list had drifted into.  The ladder runs from the cellars under the
# city out through Shadow to the Courts of Chaos and the Abyss beyond them.
#
# === Understanding dungeon.txt ===
#
# name       : the dungeon's name, as the player sees it
# depth      : shallowest : deepest.  A dungeon ends at its deepest level; to
#              go further down you must find another dungeon that reaches
#              deeper.  Deliberately: it is what the world is for.
# place      : rarity : population : height.  Rarity weights how often a world
#              holds this dungeon at all.  Population and height say what
#              country its entrance is found in -- 0 is empty wilderness or
#              sea level, 255 is a city or a mountain peak.
# floor      : the terrain its floors are made of, by name from terrain.txt.
#              Must carry the FLOOR flag, or nothing can be dropped on it and
#              no stair can be placed; "mud" is deliberately not used, since it
#              is heavy going and unlit and a whole dungeon of it is a punishment
#              rather than a character.
# profile    : the cave profile it prefers, by name from dungeon_profile.txt.
#              Omit to let the ordinary rules choose.
# desc       : one line, shown on arriving.
