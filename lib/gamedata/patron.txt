# File: patron.txt
#
# The Lords of the Courts of Chaos, whom a Chaos-Warrior serves (ZangbandTK,
# PLR-05).  See DEC-38 for why these nine and not Zangband's sixteen: its roster
# was Moorcock's Elric gods plus four Warhammer ones, and not one of them had
# anything to do with Amber.
#
# Two kinds of record.  `reward:` defines what a favour or a punishment does,
# once; `patron:` names a Lord and lists twenty rewards by code.  The list is a
# severity ladder running worst to best -- slot 1 is the Lord at its most
# offended and slot 20 at its most generous -- and the roll that indexes it
# normally avoids the bottom four.  That is Zangband's structure, kept because it
# is what makes a patron feel like a mood rather than a dice table.
#
# === Fields ===
# reward:<code>:<message>    the code rewards are referenced by, and what is said
# reward-effect:...          as elsewhere; several may follow
# reward-dice:<dice>
# reward-expr:<name>:<base>:<expr>
#
# patron:<name>
# patron-title:<title>
# patron-desc:<text>
# patron-rewards:<code>|<code>|...   exactly twenty, worst first


########## What a Lord can do to you ##########

# Every reward is Zangband's own, from gain_level_reward() in xtra2.c, with its
# words and its arithmetic (DEC-167).  They were first built from the patron
# spoiler, which named them and said little more, and most came out milder
# than the source: a WRATH that only hurt, a PISS_OFF that only frightened, a
# CHAOS_WP that was any object at all.  The exceptions are deliberate and are
# DEC-38's: POLY_SLF is a 4.2 shapechange rather than Zangband's permanent
# polymorph, and POLY_BEAST and POLY_WOLF are two more shapes of ours.  None
# has a favourite stat; Zangband's Lords leaned one stat in three towards
# their own, and ours are not Zangband's Lords.
#
# Two of Zangband's thirty-one are not here.  TY_CURSE needs no rung of its
# own, because WRATH and PISS_OFF both lay the Ancient Curse; DO_HAVOC is
# Call Chaos, which asks where to aim, and a reward cannot.

reward:IGNORE:%s ignores you.

# Zangband's worst: the hit, every stat drained, a greater summoning, the
# Ancient Curse, and an even chance each of a cursed weapon and cursed armour.
reward:WRATH:The voice of %s thunders: 'Die, mortal!'
reward-effect:DAMAGE
reward-dice:$B
reward-expr:B:PLAYER_LEVEL:* 4
reward-effect:RUIN_STATS
reward-effect:HI_SUMMON
reward-effect:ANCIENT_CURSE
reward-effect:ONE_IN
reward-dice:2
reward-effect:CURSE_WEAPON
reward-effect:ONE_IN
reward-dice:2
reward-effect:CURSE_ARMOR

# A ball around you that hits everything near you, and the same blow to you.
# Zangband's was disintegration, which this game does not have; mana is its
# nearest, pure damage that no monster resists and that destroys what lies on
# the floor.  Like Zangband's, it leaves the walls alone.

# Zangband's loop calls between two and six, re-rolling its bound on every pass,

# The three gifts that hand you something living (PLR-28).  Zangband's
