# File: patron.txt
#
# The Lords of the Courts of Chaos, whom a Chaos-Warrior serves (ZangbandTK,
# PLR-05).  See DEC-38 for why these nine and not Zangband's sixteen: its roster
# was Moorcock's Elric gods plus four Warhammer ones, and not one of them had
# anything to do with Amber.
#
# Two kinds of record.  `reward:` defines what a favour or a punishment does,
# once; `patron:` names a Lord and lists twenty rewards by code.  The list is a
# severity ladder running worst to best -- slot 1 is the Lord at its most
# offended and slot 20 at its most generous -- and the roll that indexes it
# normally avoids the bottom four.  That is Zangband's structure, kept because it
# is what makes a patron feel like a mood rather than a dice table.
#
# === Fields ===
# reward:<code>:<message>    the code rewards are referenced by, and what is said
# reward-effect:...          as elsewhere; several may follow
# reward-dice:<dice>
# reward-expr:<name>:<base>:<expr>
#
# patron:<name>
# patron-title:<title>
# patron-desc:<text>
# patron-rewards:<code>|<code>|...   exactly twenty, worst first
