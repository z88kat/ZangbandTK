# File: realm.txt

# name: realm name
# virtues: the two virtues practising this realm moves (PLR-19)
# stat: primary spellcasting stat
# verb: verb used for creating a magic effect
# spell-noun: name of magic effects
# book-noun: type of book used
# description: the realm described, for whoever is choosing one -- the
#   character creation window shows it above this class's books in it.  Lines
#   are joined, a continuation starting with a space; a line that does not
#   start with one begins a new paragraph.  The opening of the realm's section
#   in the manual (docs/realms.rst).

# Seven realms, which is Zangband's number and Zangband's list (PLR-09).
#
# Four of them are Angband 4.2's own, renamed where Zangband's name for the
# same thing is the one this game uses everywhere else (PLR-10 keeps the
# metadata -- the casting stat, verb and nouns are 4.2's and are better than
# Zangband's, which expressed them as scattered per-class constants):
#
#     4.2 arcane  -> Arcane     4.2 nature  -> Nature
#     4.2 divine  -> Life       4.2 shadow  -> Death
#
# The mapping is by content and not by name, and one of the four is worth
# reading twice. **This game's Arcane is not Zangband's Arcane.** Zangband's is
# explicitly the weak generalist -- "it has no ultra-powerful high level
# spells... all Arcane spellbooks can be bought in town"
# ([arcane.txt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220420164306/http://www.zangband.org/spoilers/arcane.txt))
# -- where 4.2's arcane realm carries Magic Missile, two books of attacks and a
# dungeon-only Wizard's Tome of Power. Folding them together makes Arcane
# stronger here than it was there. The alternative was two realms sharing one
# name, which PLR-09's count forbids. See DEC-49.
#
# Realms are not written to the savefile -- books are matched by tval and sval,
# and the realm is read for display and for calc_mana() -- so renaming a record
# costs nothing but the `book:` lines that name it.

name:arcane
description:The weak generalist, and the one realm where this game and Angband
description: disagree outright. Detection, escape, the four temporary resistances,
description: cures for poison and for light and medium wounds, Stone to Mud, Satisfy
description: Hunger, Recharging -- everything a delver wants and almost nothing that
description: kills. Its whole offence is Zap, a weak lightning bolt; Ray of Light;
description: and Elemental Ball, which throws fire, lightning, frost or acid and does
description: not let you choose which.
virtues:ENCHANT | KNOWLEDGE
stat:INT
verb:cast
spell-noun:spell
book-noun:magic book

name:life
description:The healing realm, and the one Zangband's own spoiler calls "'good'
description: magic". It is built on cures, blessings and protection, and the handful
description: of attacks in it are there for a single purpose: harming and banishing
description: foul things. No other realm keeps a character alive as well, and no
description: other realm is so uninterested in anything that is not evil.
virtues:VITALITY | COMPASSION
stat:WIS
verb:recite
spell-noun:prayer
book-noun:prayer book

name:nature
description:The weather at the top and the ground at the bottom, with healing,
description: protection and a pair of bolts in between. It holds the only powerful
description: healing spell outside Life: Herbal Healing mends a thousand points and
description: cures stunning, bleeding and poison with it, and no realm but Life has
description: anything of the size. That one spell is a large part of why a character
description: who cannot study Life studies this instead.
virtues:NATURE | HARMONY
stat:WIS
verb:chant
spell-noun:verse
book-noun:nature book

name:death
description:The foulest of them, and the one that charges you in blood. Chaos
description: punishes a miscast too, but Death is the realm where the spells that
description: work cost you something: Hellfire takes fifty to a hundred hit points to
description: cast, Genocide takes 1d4 for every creature it removes and Mass Genocide
description: 1d3, and a failed cast anywhere in the realm draws blood on its own
description: account. Zangband says it plainly -- the most powerful spells "need
description: their own blood as the focus".
virtues:UNLIFE | CHANCE
stat:INT
verb:perform
spell-noun:ritual
book-noun:shadow book

# And the three Zangband realms 4.2 has no counterpart for at all.
#
# Sorcery is the meta realm: "utility spells such as detection and identify,
# protection spells, spells for fleeing and spells to enhance your odds in
# combat. It does not, however, have any direct attacking spells."
# Enchantment and knowledge are what it moves, as arcane does -- it is the
# scholar's realm without the fire.
name:sorcery
description:The meta-realm, and the first of the three new ones to arrive.
description: Detection, identification, protection, escape, and every convenience a
description: dungeon delver wants -- and not one direct attack spell in it. That is
description: Sorcery's whole character: it makes everything else you do easier and
description: kills nothing itself.
virtues:KNOWLEDGE | ENCHANT
stat:INT
verb:invoke
spell-noun:working
book-noun:sorcery book

# Chaos is the destructive one, and the reason a Chaos-Warrior is what it is.
# Casting it courts the Courts: V_CHANCE is the virtue mutations move too.
name:chaos
description:Sorcery's opposite in every respect. Sorcery has no attack spell in it;
description: Chaos is almost nothing else. The spoiler calls it "the very element of
description: unmaking", and what it offers instead of protection is an arsenal that
description: runs from Magic Missile in the first book to Mana Storm and Call the
description: Void in the last. Chaos has no protective spells at all -- that is the
description: realm's own description of itself, and it is the thing to weigh before
description: taking it as your only one.
virtues:CHANCE | INDIVIDUALISM
stat:INT
verb:unleash
spell-noun:invocation
book-noun:chaos book

# Trump is the Amber realm, and the one this game has the strongest claim on:
# its whole conceit is a deck of cards that reaches people and places. Zangband
# took it from Zelazny and it is the only realm that arrives here already at
# home.
name:trump
description:The last realm to arrive, and the one that had to wait for pets. Fifteen
description: of its thirty-two spells summon a creature, and in Zangband every one of
description: them turns on the same question: cast it well and the creature is yours,
description: cast it badly and an angry group of them appears. Until monsters could
description: take sides, all fifteen would have come out as the bad half -- Trump
description: Cyberdemon would have been sixty mana spent to put a hostile Cyberdemon
description: next to you, which is not a weaker spell but the opposite spell. Pets
description: closed that, and the realm went in whole.
virtues:INDIVIDUALISM | KNOWLEDGE
stat:INT
verb:read
spell-noun:card
book-noun:deck
