Zangband: Flags Help

What this page shows

Every row is something that can protect you, help you or hold you back. Every column is where it can come from: the twelve things you can wear, then You (your race, class and level), then Timed (potions, scrolls and spells, which wear off).

The Result column, next to each name, is what the row adds up to. Start there: it answers "am I protected against this?" without you adding anything up.

Getting an explanation

Point at anything. The status bar at the bottom of the Character window explains it straight away. Keep still for a moment and a card shows the whole story: for a row, every source and the answer; for a heading, what taking that item off would change.

Finding your way

  • Show picks the rows: All, In effect (what you have, good or bad), Against you (vulnerabilities and drawbacks) or Not yet learned.
  • Click a band such as Resistances to fold that group away. Click it again to open it.
  • Click a row's name, or a column's heading, to mark it while you read across or down. Click again to clear the mark.
  • Double-click a heading to inspect that item.
  • F1 on the Flags page opens this window. Esc closes it.

Each cell says what that item, you or a timed effect does for the row. Every mark has its own shape as well as its own colour, so the page reads the same in the colour-blind palette.

Three rules worth knowing

  • A vulnerability cancels a resistance, however many items resist. Immunity is the exception: it survives a vulnerability.
  • For now means a timed effect is supplying it. When the effect wears off, the Result goes back to what your equipment gives you.
  • Unknown means nothing you know of gives it, but something you wear has a property you haven't identified, and it could be this one.

Worn items

The first twelve columns are your equipment slots, in the game's order: Weapon, Bow, Ring R (right hand), Ring L (left hand), Amulet, Light, Body, Cloak, Shield, Helm, Gloves and Boots. Each heading shows the item's symbol in its own colour.

You and Timed

You is what your race, class and level give you. Nothing can take these away, and you can't take them off. Timed is what potions, scrolls and spells are giving you right now. It wears off on its own.

What would I lose?

Point at an item's heading. Its card lists what you'd lose, what you'd gain and what wouldn't change if you took it off. A cursed item can be holding something back, so taking it off may gain you more than it costs.

If the card says taking it off would change nothing here, other things you wear already give you all of it, and that slot is free for something better. In a row's card, "Two things give it" tells you the same thing from the other direction.

Runes you haven't learned

Every property on this page is a rune. You learn a rune the first time you notice it working, whether it comes from an item or from yourself. Until then:

  • its name on this page is grey and slanted;
  • an item that might have it shows ? in that row. The game can tell that an item has a rune you haven't learned, but not which one, so the ? goes on every row whose rune you haven't learned;
  • everything else on the row is still true. What your race, class and level give you, and anything from items you've identified, shows as normal.

Show: Not yet learned lists just these rows.

A new character

A new character knows very few runes, so most of the page starts out grey. That's expected. It fills in as you play, and nothing on it is hidden from you that the game would otherwise tell you.
