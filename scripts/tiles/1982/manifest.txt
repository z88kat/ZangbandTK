# manifest.txt -- which 1982 tile each thing in the game is drawn with.
#
# Read by scripts/tiles/build-1982.py.  A tile is named by its id in the
# source atlas (source.txt), which is the id it had in the brief.  Monsters
# are not here: monsters.txt lists every one, because which of them has a
# picture of its own was decided one by one.
#
# A recolourable tile (source.txt says `recolour`) is drawn in four greys and
# tinted with the thing's own colour from lib/gamedata, so one potion bottle
# is every potion and one young dragon every young dragon.
#
# Anything not listed here and not covered by a `tval:` default has no tile
# and renders as its ASCII letter; audit-tiles.py reports it.

# --- terrain: feat:<CODE>:<tile>[:<ground>][:<stamp>][:outline] ---
#
# <ground> is what shows through a tile drawn with transparency: the floor for
# rubble, the town's grass for the buildings drawn on magenta.  <stamp> is the
# number or letter stamped on a building's gable -- the shop's glyph from
# terrain.txt, or for the services, whose glyph is +, a letter the owner chose
# on 3 November 2026.  `outline` gives the tile the figures' ink outline before
# it goes onto its ground: the Chaos Tower, lightened a step, lost its own.
